The story behind 52Hz

The whale known as the 52Hz whale became widely discussed because its recorded call was measured at around 52 hertz — a frequency different from the calls commonly associated with many other whale populations.

Its story has captured people's imagination. It reminds us that the natural world is full of voices, patterns and lives that do not always fit what we expect.

At 7ECLIPSES, the 52Hz whale is not a symbol of sadness. It is a reminder that being different is not something you need to apologise for. You do not have to change your voice, hide your feelings or become a copy of someone else just to belong.

Be yourself — with your own pace, your own ideas, your own little imperfections. And while you are becoming who you want to be, remember to offer that same kindness to the people around you.
