A story from the deep

The whale who sang at 52Hz.

In the vastness of the ocean, a whale was heard singing at 52 hertz — a frequency different from the calls usually heard from other whales. The story became a symbol of being different, of searching for connection, and of continuing to sing anyway.

That is the spirit behind 7ECLIPSES. We believe your difference is not something to hide. Your voice, your pace, your story — they deserve space.
