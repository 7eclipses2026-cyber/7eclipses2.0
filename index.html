A story from the deep

A frequency of its own.

The 52Hz whale became known because its recorded call was at a frequency different from the calls usually heard from other whales. Its story has inspired people to think about individuality and the beauty of being different.

For 7ECLIPSES, 52Hz is a reminder that you do not have to change who you are just to fit a pattern. Your voice, your pace and your way of becoming deserve space.

A little of what we believe
