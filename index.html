A story from the deep

A frequency of its own.

The 52Hz whale became known because its recorded call was at a frequency different from the calls usually heard from other whales. Its story has inspired people to think about individuality and the beauty of being different.

For 7ECLIPSES, 52Hz is a reminder that you do not have to change who you are just to fit a pattern. Your voice, your pace and your way of becoming deserve space.

Find your fit

Drop-shoulder size guide.

Our unisex tees are designed with a relaxed, loose fit, dropped shoulders and extra room through the chest. Compare the garment measurements below with a tee you already love.

SizeChest (around)LengthShoulder
S40"27"20"
M42"28"21"
L44"29"22"
XL46"30"23"

All measurements are suggested garment measurements in inches (not body measurements). Chest is the full garment circumference; shoulder is measured seam-to-seam. Please compare with your production sample before publishing the chart as final.

A little fit advice

Room to move. Made to drape.

Choose your usual size for a relaxed oversized silhouette. Size down for a neater loose fit, or size up for an extra-roomy streetwear look. If you are between sizes, choose based on your preferred fit.

The product size selector uses the same S–XL size range.
