Terms & Conditions

These terms explain the basics of shopping with 7ECLIPSES, including orders, payments, cancellations, returns, exchanges, delivery and how customer information is handled. Please read them before placing an order.

1. Order placement and payment

  • Please review the product, selected colour, size, quantity, delivery details and total shown at checkout before confirming.
  • An order is considered placed only after the website displays an order confirmation or provides an order ID.
  • Product availability can change. An item in your bag is not reserved until the order is successfully placed.
  • Use only the payment options displayed at checkout. Never share your password, OTP, card PIN, CVV or UPI PIN with anyone, including someone claiming to be from 7ECLIPSES.
  • If payment is deducted but no order confirmation appears, do not repeatedly retry the payment. Contact support with the payment reference.

2. Cancellation policy

If you need to cancel an order, contact us as soon as possible with your order ID. A cancellation request is not a confirmation that the order has been cancelled. If the order has already been processed or dispatched, cancellation may no longer be possible. Our team will confirm the status and any applicable next steps.

3. Return and exchange policy

  • For a return or exchange request, contact us within 3–4 days of receiving the order.
  • For a size or preference-based exchange, a ₹60 exchange handling/delivery charge applies.
  • Items should be unused, unwashed, undamaged and returned with their original tags and packaging. Please contact us before sending anything back so the team can guide you.
  • Returns or exchanges are subject to inspection and confirmation by our team. Do not send an item to an address that has not been confirmed by support.
  • Share your order ID and explain whether you need a return or an exchange. The team will confirm eligibility and the process.

4. Damaged, defective or wrong products

If your order arrives damaged, defective or different from what you ordered, contact us promptly with your order ID, clear photos and, if available, an unboxing video. Please keep the product and packaging until support advises you. Our team will review the issue and guide you through the appropriate resolution.

5. Shipping and delivery

Delivery charges, where applicable, are shown during checkout. Any delivery estimate is indicative and may be affected by courier operations, location, weather or other circumstances outside our control. For an update on a placed order, contact support with your order ID. We do not want to promise a delivery date that has not been confirmed by the courier.

6. Refund policy

Refund eligibility is reviewed according to the circumstances of the order and the applicable return or cancellation outcome. Once a refund is approved, the processing time can depend on the payment provider and your bank. If a payment was deducted but the order was not confirmed, contact support with the payment reference before making another payment. Our team will advise you on the next step.

7. Contact and customer support

For order questions, cancellation requests, delivery updates, returns, exchanges or payment concerns, contact the 7ECLIPSES support team. Include your order ID where applicable so we can help more quickly.
